# YouTube Settings (New)

Stream Toolkit connects your YouTube channel by signing in with Google, so there is no API key to create or enter.

## Step 1: Connect YouTube Account

1. Open Stream Toolkit
2. Click **Settings** in the bottom left menu
3. Find **YouTube** under Platform Connections and click **Connect Account**
4. A confirmation window opens. Read the YouTube Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click **Agree and sign in**
5. The browser opens the Google sign-in page. Choose the Google account that owns your YouTube channel (if the account has several channels, pick the one you stream on)
6. Review the requested permissions and click **Continue** / **Allow**

Once done, the YouTube row in Settings shows **Connected: channel name**.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** next to YouTube. This also removes Stream Toolkit's access from your Google account.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I still need a YouTube API key?**
No. Signing in with Google is all you need.

**Q: I signed in with the wrong channel**
Click **Disconnect**, then connect again and choose the correct account or channel.

**Q: Nothing happens after signing in in the browser**
Keep Stream Toolkit open while you sign in — the browser sends the result back to it. If it still does not update, click Connect Account again.
